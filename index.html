FROM PLAN TO WALL

Your hanging guide

A wall, well put together.

  1. Measure your wall. Enter its width and height. Add a photo and mark the four corners of that same rectangle.
  2. Add your pieces. Pick each shape and enter outside dimensions, frame included. Add a photo if you like.
  3. Try an arrangement. Pick a layout and set the spacing. Every arrangement preserves your actual sizes.
  4. Make it your own. Drag pieces or tap to edit an exact position. Arrow keys move a focused piece; Enter edits it.
  5. Take it to the wall. Open the hanging guide for edge and center measurements. Your latest wall restores automatically on this device. Use Save project for a downloadable backup. Finish piece edits with Save changes; use Save & update when an update is ready.

This planner uses measured geometry, so automatic layouts work without an AI subscription. Photos are previews; verify dimensions with a tape measure. Hanging points depend on your actual hardware.
