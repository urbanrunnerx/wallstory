How to measure the hardware

Keep the frame’s intended top at the top and turn it over left to right. Measure to the spot where the nail or hook actually supports the hanger, from the frame’s outside edges.

For wire, pull it taut at the intended support points and measure under tension. For a wall hook, measure how far its nail entry is above its support point. This editor supports one nail directly above each hook; use separately measured positions for other hardware.

Measure against the wall rectangle you entered. The photo and screen size are previews, not measuring tools.
